## Introduction

Welcome to NaioshFit, an AI-powered fitness platform that helps users achieve their health and fitness goals. This guide will help you understand how to use the platform based on your account type.

### What is NaioshFit?

NaioshFit is a comprehensive fitness platform that offers:
- **Personalized Training Plans**: AI-powered workout recommendations
- **Nutrition Tracking**: Meal logging and macro tracking
- **Progress Monitoring**: Weight, measurements, and performance tracking
- **Educational Courses**: Learn about fitness, nutrition, and wellness
- **Community Features**: Connect with others on similar fitness journeys
- **E-Commerce**: Purchase supplements and fitness products
- **AI Assistant**: Get instant answers to fitness questions
- **Multi-Language Support**: Available in English and Arabic

## Account Types Overview

NaioshFit has five main account types, each with different capabilities:

| Account Type | Primary Use | Key Capabilities |
|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| **User/Trainee** | Personal fitness journey | Track workouts, meals, progress; access courses; use AI assistant |
| **Coach** | Train and manage clients | Create plans, monitor trainees, provide guidance, earn from courses |

## User/Trainee Guide

### Overview

As a User/Trainee, you're here to achieve your fitness goals. The platform provides tools to track your journey, learn, and stay motivated.

### Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Today's Summary**: Calories, macros, workouts scheduled
- **Quick Actions**: Log meal, start workout, check in
- **Progress Graph**: Weight and body composition trends
- **Upcoming Workouts**: Scheduled training sessions
- **Notifications**: Reminders, achievements, messages from coach

---

### Getting Started as a User

#### Step 1: Complete Your Profile

1. Click your **profile picture** in the top-right
2. Select **"Edit Profile"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Fitness Goal**: Weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, etc.
   - **Training Level**: Beginner, intermediate, advanced
   - **Medical Conditions**: Any health concerns (private, only visible to coach)
   - **Dietary Preferences**: Vegetarian, vegan, no restrictions
   - **Allergies**: Food allergies or intolerances
   - **Available Equipment**: Home gym, full gym, bodyweight only
   - **Training Schedule**: Days and times available for workouts
4. Upload a **profile picture**
5. Click **"Save Changes"**

#### Step 2: Set Your Goals

1. Go to **Settings → Goals**
2. Set your targets:
   - **Target Weight**: Your weight goal
   - **Target Date**: When you want to achieve it
   - **Daily Calories**: Auto-calculated or custom
   - **Macro Split**: Protein, carbs, fats percentages
   - **Weekly Workouts**: How many workouts per week
   - **Daily Steps**: Step goal (e.g., 10,000)
   - **Water Intake**: Glasses per day (e.g., 8)
3. Click **"Save Goals"**

#### Step 3: Choose Your Coach (Optional)

1. Go to **"Find a Coach"**
2. Browse available coaches:
   - View profiles, specialties, and reviews
   - Filter by language, gender, experience
3. Click **"Request Coach"** on your preferred coach
4. Wait for acceptance (usually within 24 hours)
5. Once accepted, your coach can:
   - Create custom workout plans
   - Design meal plans
   - Monitor your progress
   - Message you directly

### Daily Workflow

#### Morning Routine

**1. Check Today's Plan**
- Open the app/website
- Review scheduled workouts
- Check nutrition targets
- Read any messages from coach

**2. Pre-Workout**
- Log your **pre-workout meal**:
  1. Click **"Add Meal"**
  2. Select **"Breakfast"** and time
  3. Search foods from database or create custom
  4. Set quantities
  5. View calculated macros
  6. Click **"Save Meal"**

**3. Complete Your Workout**
- Go to **"Today's Workouts"**
- Click **"Start Workout"** on assigned workout
- Follow exercise list:
  - View exercise demo videos
  - Log sets, reps, weight used
  - Rest timer between sets
  - Notes for each exercise
- Click **"Complete Workout"** when done
- Rate difficulty (1-5 stars)
- Add workout notes

**4. Post-Workout**
- Log your **post-workout meal**
- Log your **weight** (if you weigh daily):
  1. Go to **"Progress"**
  2. Click **"Add Entry"**
  3. Enter weight, date, time
  4. Add notes if needed

#### Throughout the Day

**Log All Meals**
- Log lunch, dinner, snacks
- Use **Quick Add** for common meals
- Scan barcodes (mobile app) for packaged foods
- View macro progress bar to stay on target

**Track Water Intake**
- Click the **water drop icon**
- Increment each time you drink a glass
- Get reminders to stay hydrated

**Track Steps**
- Sync fitness tracker (Apple Health, Google Fit)
- Or manually log steps

**Use AI Assistant**
- Have questions? Ask the AI:
  - "What should I eat before my workout?"
  - "Is it okay to skip cardio today?"
  - "How do I increase my protein intake?"
- Get instant, personalized answers

#### Evening Routine

**1. Complete Daily Check-In**
- Go to **"Daily Stats"**
- Review your day:
  - Calories consumed vs. target
  - Macro breakdown
  - Workouts completed
  - Steps taken
  - Water consumed
- Add daily notes about how you felt

**2. Take Progress Photos (Weekly)**
- Go to **"Progress Photos"**
- Take front, side, back photos
- Compare with previous weeks
- Keep photos private or share with coach

**3. Set Tomorrow's Intentions**
- Review tomorrow's workout
- Plan meals ahead
- Set reminders

### Features Deep Dive

**Adding a Meal**
1. Go to **"Nutrition"** tab

   - **My Recipes**: Save custom recipes

5. Adjust serving sizes
6. Review totals (calories, protein, carbs, fats, fiber)
7. Click **"Save Meal"**

**Creating a Recipe**
1. Go to **"My Recipes"**
2. Click **"New Recipe"**
3. Enter recipe name
4. Add ingredients with quantities
5. Set number of servings
6. Macros calculated automatically
7. Save and use in future meals

**Meal Plans from Coach**
- If your coach creates meal plans:
  1. Go to **"Meal Plans"**
  2. View weekly plan
  3. Click **"Use This Meal"** to log it
  4. Adjust portions if needed

#### Workout Tracking

**Assigned Workouts**
- Your coach assigns workouts to specific days
- View them in **"My Workouts"**
- Each workout includes:
  - Exercise name with demo video/GIF
  - Sets and reps
  - Rest periods
  - Coach notes
  - Previous performance (to beat)

**Starting a Workout**
1. Click **"Start Workout"**
2. For each exercise:
   - Read instructions
   - Watch demo (tap exercise name)
   - Complete first set
   - Click **"Add Set"**
   - Enter weight and reps completed
   - Rest timer starts automatically
   - Repeat for all sets
3. Add exercise notes if needed
4. Move to next exercise
5. Complete all exercises
6. Click **"Finish Workout"**
7. Rate difficulty and add overall notes

**Custom Workouts**
- Not following a plan? Create your own:
  1. Go to **"Quick Add Workout"**
  2. Enter workout name
  3. Add exercises from library
  4. Log your performance
  5. Save for future reference

**Cardio Tracking**
- Log running, cycling, swimming:
  1. Select cardio type
  2. Enter duration or distance
  3. Log pace, heart rate (optional)
  4. Calories burned calculated automatically

#### Step 1: Coach Application & Approval

**Initial Signup**
1. Create account selecting **"Coach"** role

**Option 3: AI-Assisted Program Design**
1. Click **"AI Program Generator"**
2. Enter parameters:

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Forgot Password**
1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on login page
2. Enter email address
3. Receive reset link via email
4. Click link and set new password
5. Login with new password

**Account Locked**
- After 5 failed login attempts, account locks for 15 minutes
- Wait 15 minutes or use password reset

**Can't Receive Reset Email**
- Check spam folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Add support@naioshfit.com to contacts
- Contact support if still not received

### Syncing Issues

**Data Not Syncing**
1. Check internet connection
2. Force refresh (pull down on mobile)
3. Logout and login again
4. Clear app cache (mobile)
5. Reinstall app (last resort)

**Fitness Tracker Not Syncing**
1. Check permissions (app needs access)
2. Reconnect tracker:
   - Settings → Integrations
   - Disconnect tracker
   - Reconnect tracker
3. Ensure tracker app is updated
4. Restart both apps

### Payment Issues

**Payment Declined**
- Verify card information
- Check with bank (possible fraud alert)
- Try different card
- Contact support for manual payment

**Missing Credits After Purchase**
- Wait 5 minutes (processing time)
- Check transaction history
- Contact support with receipt

**Subscription Not Renewing**
- Update payment method in Settings
- Check if subscription cancelled
- Resubscribe if necessary

### Technical Issues

**App Crashing**
1. Update app to latest version
2. Restart device
3. Clear app cache
4. Reinstall app
5. Report crash to support (with device info)

**Slow Performance**
- Check internet speed
- Close other apps
- Clear browser cache (web)
- Update app/browser

**Missing Features**
- Verify subscription tier (some features are paid)
- Check if feature disabled by admin/gym
- Update app (new features may require latest version)
